The app now remembers which sources you show.

## Saved selections

- Namespace checkboxes and sources you toggle one at a time are saved, separately for each server, and come back the next time you open the app.
- A namespace's choice applies to its pods as they're replaced, since pod names change with every rollout. Turn off `kube-system` once and it stays off.
- A pod toggled on its own keeps its choice over its namespace, until you click the namespace's checkbox again.
- **Tail only this** and **Reset filters** stay temporary, so a quick look at one source doesn't overwrite your setup.
- Sources without a saved choice start as before: on if they're running.

The choices live in `~/Library/Application Support/telelogs/selections.json` on macOS; delete it to start fresh.

## Install

Same as [v0.3.0](https://github.com/tolaniverse/telelogs/releases/tag/v0.3.0): `cargo install --locked telelogs` and `telelog-server`, `ghcr.io/tolaniverse/telelog-server:0.3.1`, or the server archives and the macOS app below, checked against `SHA256SUMS`. The app isn't notarized yet; the first time, click **Open Anyway** in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
